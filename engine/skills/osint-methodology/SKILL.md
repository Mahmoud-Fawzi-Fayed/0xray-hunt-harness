# OSINT Methodology

Complete source-specific procedures, asset types, investigation playbooks, and templates: [references/full-guide.md](references/full-guide.md).

## Pipeline

Seed discovery → asset expansion → enrichment → exposure analysis → reporting.

Keep an evidence-backed asset graph with provenance, confidence, scope, and timestamps. Separate passive research from active target traffic, route active requests through the scope-guarded transport, and never infer a finding from exposure alone.
