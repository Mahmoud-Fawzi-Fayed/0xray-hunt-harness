# Report Writing

Templates, tone guidance, severity examples, and downgrade counters: [references/full-guide.md](references/full-guide.md).

## Required structure

- Specific title and impact-first summary.
- Numbered steps with exact redacted request and response evidence.
- The attacker, affected users, and demonstrated harm — never theoretical impact.
- Reproduction method, dedup verdict/differentiator, CVSS vector, and concrete remediation.

Do not draft with unresolved reproduction, scope, or dedup status. Submission always requires per-report human approval.
